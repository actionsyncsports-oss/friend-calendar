Tap a day to add your free time or see who else is open. Each circle is a friend who’s free sometime that day, in their own colour (a dashed ring means only “open”, and yours has a dark outline). A “+3” means three more than fit. Friends are listed by who you end up doing things with most, then whose free time is most like yours. A yellow day means everyone you’re counting is free at the same time. “3 of 5” is the best overlap that day. The ◆ marks a plan you’re in, and a small ring marks an event a friend added.
Your crew and groups come from ActionSync. Free time, plans and messages are still local to this device — syncing those is next.
